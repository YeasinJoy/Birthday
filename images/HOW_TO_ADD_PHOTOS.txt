📸 HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN PHOTOS (PART 1: HER • PART 2: US)
==============================================================================

Simply copy/paste your photos into this "images" folder!

Suggested filenames:
  - images/her1.jpg, her2.jpg, her3.jpg  (Solo pictures of her)
  - images/us1.jpg, us2.jpg, us3.jpg    (Photos of the two of you together)

Next step:
Open config.js in Notepad or any editor:

1. In "memoriesHer" (Part 1: Her Moments):
   image: "images/her1.jpg",
   caption: "Quiet grace and genuine smiles.",
   tag: "Radiant ✨"

2. In "memoriesUs" (Part 2: Moments Of Us):
   image: "images/us1.jpg",
   caption: "One of those evenings you never want to end.",
   tag: "Memories Together ☕"

💡 REUSING FOR FRIENDS LATER:
In config.js:
1. Change recipientName: "Friend's Name"
2. Change birthdayDate: "Month Day"
3. Set relationshipType: "friendship"
4. Put their solo pictures in memoriesHer and shared pictures in memoriesUs!
Everything else updates automatically.
